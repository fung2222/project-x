# ---- OPTION A (RECOMMENDED): one line, works in ANY host timezone, DST/holiday/weekday-proof.

# close 17:00 ET, morning 08:30 HKT Tue-Sat, weekly Mon 09:44 HKT) and then the watchdog (re-run / 1 alert).
* * * * * /opt/data/project-x/ops/hermes/px_job.sh tick
